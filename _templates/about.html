About AliGlobalShop

Our Mission

AliGlobalShop finds and curates the best deals on AliExpress, updated daily, ranked by real sales data, discount percentage, and customer ratings. No paid placements, no sponsored posts.

How We Make Money

We are an AliExpress affiliate. When you click a product link and complete a purchase, we earn a commission of 3–9% of the sale price. This comes at zero extra cost to you: the price you pay is exactly the same as going directly to AliExpress.

Our income depends entirely on recommending products that are actually worth buying. We have no incentive to push overpriced or low-quality items.

How Products Are Selected

  • Ranked by a score combining sales volume, discount %, and customer rating
  • Maximum price: $200 USD per item
  • Minimum discount: 5%
  • Filtered to remove irrelevant categories
  • Images re-hosted on Cloudinary CDN for fast global delivery
